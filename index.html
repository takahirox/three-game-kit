three-game-kitBrowser showcases built from the kit's public Features. Desktop keyboard + mouse; click a game to capture the pointer.

Every showcase below runs entirely in your browser. Most paint their textures and synthesise their sounds at runtime, so there is nothing to download besides the code.

  • CraftlandsMinecraft-inspired survival sandbox: infinite streamed world, flood-fill lighting, crafting, furnaces, chests, beds, mobs, day / night, synthesised sound.SURVIVAL SANDBOX
  • DeepfieldA hand-sized voxel world of grass, stone, caves and ore with a five-step expedition log and autosave.VOXEL SANDBOX
  • Relic FrontierThe official integrated showcase: a third-person action adventure with energy cells, upgrades, a guardian fight and an escape.ACTION ADVENTURE
  • Chroma StrikeRelic Frontier's first-person combat slice with a glTF pulse rifle and combat bots.FPS SLICE
  • GravetideFive-minute survivor run: auto-firing weapons, soul gems, level-up upgrades and an ever-growing graveyard tide.SURVIVOR RUN
  • AfterglowNeon survival sprint down a floating highway: jump gaps, thread laser gates, manage boost heat, beat your ghost.ARCADE RACER
  • Core RunThe minimal one-runtime showcase that exercises the Core scheduling and lifecycle contracts.CORE DEMO
  • Particle atlasTwenty composed particle effects demonstrating the reusable emitter API.VFX WORKSHOP
Source and documentation: github.com/takahirox/three-game-kit
